# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Content production teams and strategy reviewers use the report to turn an industry Top1000 ranking and selected material-level trend data into concrete creative directions. They scan findings quickly, compare material patterns, and inspect supporting evidence before planning tests.

## Product Purpose

This project generates an offline, interactive industry content analysis report. It combines Top1000 material performance data with second-by-second diagnostics for selected materials, making both the market-level pattern and the creative-level evidence available in one deliverable.

## Brand Personality

Professional, restrained, data-dense. The report should feel like a trustworthy working document for experienced content teams: clear hierarchy, decisive comparisons, and visual evidence without decorative noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing landing-page composition, oversized decorative metrics, long narrative card stacks, opaque AI-generated conclusions, and sparse dashboards that hide the supporting data. Do not sacrifice dense comparison for visual novelty.

## Design Principles

1. Keep every conclusion traceable to either Top1000 ranking data or single-material second-level data.
2. Separate market-level comparison from material-level creative diagnosis, then connect them through clear references.
3. Put comparison before narration: charts, tables, and sorted views should surface the pattern before explanatory text.
4. Make interactions reveal detail without blocking a fast first read of the report.
5. Preserve offline delivery and state data coverage, inferred fields, and missing details plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast targets, support keyboard access for all interactive controls and image previews, respect reduced-motion preferences, and retain usable content at desktop and mobile widths.
